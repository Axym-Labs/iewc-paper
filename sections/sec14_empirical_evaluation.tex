\section{Empirical Evaluation}

We use two complementary evidence tiers. The retained multi-domain study establishes IEWC's attained performance across classification, NLP, forecasting, segmentation, and diffusion; its selected endpoints are descriptive because they participated in method development. The locked study freezes selection before test access and estimates paired held-out effects. It compares IEF with OPG under matched uniform weighting and diagonal scale, then crosses OPG/IEF with uniform/RDW aggregation. Appendix C specifies data roles, fixed validation grids, repetitions, baselines, and uncertainty calculations.

Unless stated otherwise, all stored matrices are diagonal. Paper-facing EWC, IEWC, and IEWC+RDW denote observed-label OPG, uniform IEF, and residual-diversity-weighted IEF, respectively. RDW is order dependent, so its gains cannot be attributed to normalization alone; Appendix C maps the archived labels and complete protocols.

Table \ref{tab:main-empirical-results} shows that uniform IEWC attained higher selected means than OPG-EWC on ImageNet-R and VOC 15--5, while the weighted IEWC configuration attained the highest TRACE score and the lowest forecasting and DDPM MSE. The matched factorial and locked evaluation then isolates which gains generalize and which arise from normalization versus weighting.

At the distribution level, Figure~\ref{fig:distribution-retention}A--B shows that IEWC's retained accuracy is not concentrated in a single late distribution. The locked paired analysis tests whether this advantage generalizes across frozen class orders. The weighted IEWC run likewise has less forgetting on C-STANCE and FOMC in Figure~\ref{fig:distribution-retention}C--D. The locked comparison crosses the weighting mechanism with both OPG and IEF, while replacing FOMC with NumGLUE-cm to obtain disjoint validation and test roles.


Weighted IEWC retains more target-trajectory structure at the first task and remains close to OPG-EWC later; Appendix Figure~\ref{fig:forecast-rollouts} provides representative held-out rollouts alongside the aggregate scores.

The DDPM study also uses a row/column cumulative-distribution output representation motivated by sliced Wasserstein distance. The selected run reduced old-output drift to $9.4409\pm7.2517$, compared with $14.1026\pm12.2220$ for Euclidean uniform IEWC, while attaining denoising MSE $0.0301\pm0.0012$ versus $0.0298\pm0.0014$. Because that surrogate did not establish a matched primal/dual metric pair, the result supports output-representation flexibility but not a claim about Wasserstein geometry. Appendix~\ref{app:ordered-cdf-geometry} supplies a correct ordered-CDF/Cram\'er construction.

Figure~\ref{fig:locked-summands}C--F shows the effective per-sample diagonal summands, $\lambda$ times the per-sample diagonal trace before averaging, in four representative settings. In classification and regression, OPG summands span more than three orders of magnitude between their 5th and 95th percentiles, while IEWC narrows this range by more than an order of magnitude; in diffusion and segmentation, both spreads are comparable.

\subsection{Locked evaluation}

The locked study selects $\lambda$ on validation data from fixed five-point grids and evaluates each method once on held-out data (Appendix~\ref{app:confirmatory-results}). ImageNet-R uses ten paired class orders; TRACE crosses all six orders of C-STANCE, ScienceQA, and NumGLUE-cm with two seeds. On ImageNet-R, uniform IEWC improves final average accuracy over matched EWC by 2.00 percentage points (95\% CI 0.93--3.07; exact sign-flip $p=0.0078$; Figure~\ref{fig:locked-summands}A), and the IEF advantage persists under RDW aggregation (1.71 points, 0.92--2.51; Holm $p=0.016$). On TRACE, the primary difference is $-0.11$ points ($-1.48$--$1.27$; Figure~\ref{fig:locked-summands}B).
