\section{Empirical Evaluation}

We use two evidence tiers. The confirmatory comparison is frozen before locked-test access and asks whether IEF normalization helps relative to OPG under identical uniform weights and matched global diagonal scale. It uses disjoint train, validation, and locked-test roles; paired task orders and optimizer seeds; fixed five-point validation grids without edge expansion; and paired uncertainty on the held-out repetitions. The secondary $2\times2$ comparison crosses OPG/IEF with uniform/RDW aggregation. Sampled model-Fisher EWC and MAS are external regularization baselines; sequential training and offline/joint training provide lower-context and non-continual references. This comparator set preserves the same post-task diagonal-penalty interface: SI's trajectory accumulation and LwF's distillation objective are discussed in Section~2 but are not mixed into the estimator-focused factorial. Appendix C specifies the complete protocol.

The original multi-domain study is retained quantitatively in full because it establishes IEWC's empirical breadth and attained performance across classification, NLP, forecasting, segmentation, and diffusion. Its selected endpoints participated in hyperparameter and method development, so comparisons within that study are descriptive rather than selection-independent effect estimates; the new locked study supplies the latter evidence under a matched factorial design. Unless stated otherwise, all stored matrices are diagonal. Historical labels embedded in the original figures map as follows: EF-EWC denotes observed-label OPG-EWC, IEWC-E or U-IEWC denotes uniform IEF, and IEWC-GSS or GSS-IEWC denotes the earlier GSS-motivated weighting implementation. The last implementation is order dependent, so its gains cannot be attributed to normalization alone.

Table \ref{tab:main-empirical-results} shows that uniform IEWC attained higher selected means than OPG-EWC on ImageNet-R and VOC 15--5, while the weighted IEWC configuration attained the highest TRACE score and the lowest forecasting and DDPM MSE. Together, these results demonstrate broad applicability and strong selected performance. The matched factorial and locked evaluation then isolates which gains generalize and which arise from normalization versus weighting.

Weighted IEWC retains more target-trajectory structure at the first task and remains close to OPG-EWC later; these examples illustrate the observed behavior rather than replace the aggregate uncertainty estimates.

The DDPM study also tested an alternative output representation originally labeled ``sliced Wasserstein.'' The selected run reduced old-output drift to $9.4409\pm7.2517$, compared with $14.1026\pm12.2220$ for Euclidean uniform IEWC, while attaining denoising MSE $0.0301\pm0.0012$ versus $0.0298\pm0.0014$. Because that surrogate did not establish a matched primal/dual metric pair, the result supports output-representation flexibility but not a claim about Wasserstein geometry. Section~11 supplies a correct ordered-CDF/Cram\'er construction.

The FROMP-derived weighting ablation \citep{FROMP} reached $0.6365\pm0.0165$ final average accuracy and $0.3068\pm0.0170$ forgetting on ImageNet-R, but $0.3351\pm0.0742$ final all-mIoU and $0.4064\pm0.1122$ base-class forgetting on VOC 15--5. Its domain-dependent behavior further motivates treating aggregation as an explicit design axis rather than folding it into the normalization claim.
