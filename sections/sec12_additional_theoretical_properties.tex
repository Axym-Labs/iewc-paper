Our derivations concern full positive-semidefinite matrices, whereas the training experiments store only their diagonals. Taking $\operatorname{diag}(M)$ preserves each coordinate-axis quadratic penalty, nonnegativity of the entries, and the trace. It generally does not preserve off-axis quadratic forms, rank, eigenvectors, parameterization behavior, or the full normal-space geometry. Consequently, the full-matrix contamination analysis in Appendix B tests the proposed normalization mechanism, while benchmark training separately tests whether its diagonal projection is useful.

Appendix A gives the exact scope of loss-rescaling invariance and Appendix B analyzes and directly tests the contamination mechanism.
