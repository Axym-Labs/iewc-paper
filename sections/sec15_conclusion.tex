\section{Conclusion}

CLOC is a preservation-first formulation deriving local penalties from selected learned-behavior functionals. Scalar-loss preservation yields an OPG-based regularization that under NLL equals EWC's empirical-Fisher regularization, whereas preserving outputs modulo old-loss level-set tangents yields IEWC and explains its normalization geometrically. CLOC contextualizes damping, geometry, aggregation, and approximation. Mechanism tests, the multi-domain study, and the held-out evaluation show IEWC variants improve EWC in several settings and remain comparable in others.
