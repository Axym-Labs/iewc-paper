\section{Conclusion}

CLOC is a preservation-first formulation deriving local penalties from selected learned-behavior functionals. Scalar-loss preservation yields an OPG-based regularization that under NLL equals EWC's empirical-Fisher regularization, whereas preserving outputs modulo old-loss level-set tangents yields IEWC and explains its normalization geometrically. CLOC also makes damping, output geometry, aggregation, and matrix approximation explicit design choices. Mechanism tests, the multi-domain study, and the held-out evaluation show that IEWC variants match or improve on EWC, including a 2.00-point gain in held-out ImageNet-R accuracy. Choosing what to preserve, rather than which curvature to approximate, is thus the decision from which a continual-learning penalty follows.
