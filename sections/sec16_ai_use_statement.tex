\subsection*{AI Use Statement}

The original IEWC framework and initial manuscript predate the generative-AI
assistance disclosed here. For the present revision, OpenAI Codex language
models assisted with review-response planning; literature search and
summarization; checking, extending, and editing mathematical derivations;
designing the frozen confirmatory protocol; creating and editing experiment,
analysis, testing, and archival code; experiment orchestration and failure
diagnosis; generating draft tables and figures; interpreting preserved result
records; and drafting and editing manuscript text. Generative AI was not used
to create benchmark labels or examples, alter evaluation data, select outcomes
after locked-test access, conduct human-subject analysis, translate material,
or transcribe recordings.

The author retained responsibility for the research questions, protocol and
claim decisions, and final manuscript. AI-assisted code was checked through
unit and integration tests, deterministic duplicate runs, clean-checkout
reproduction, and artifact checksums. Numerical claims were regenerated from
preserved records and checked against machine-readable summaries; mathematical
claims were checked against the stated assumptions and displayed derivations.
The author takes responsibility for all content of the submission, including
AI-assisted text, code, analyses, and artifacts.
