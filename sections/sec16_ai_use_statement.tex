\subsection*{AI Use Statement}

The original IEWC framework and initial manuscript predate the generative-AI
assistance disclosed here. Generative AI tools were used to assist with revision
planning; theoretical and experimental development; implementation and
experiment execution; analysis and interpretation; figure creation; literature
search and synthesis; and manuscript drafting and editing. The author reviewed
AI-assisted code, experimental outputs, numerical claims, citations, figures,
and text against saved artifacts and executable code, and takes responsibility
for the final content.
