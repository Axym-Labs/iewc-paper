\section{Contamination Contributions and Spectral Stability}

The OPG--IEF normalization difference can change which samples dominate a full importance matrix. In Euclidean output geometry, write $s_i=\|r_i\|_2^2$ and $a_i=g_i/\sqrt{s_i}$ for $s_i>0$. The undamped per-sample matrices are
\[
M_{\mathrm{OPG},i}=s_i a_i a_i^\top,
\qquad
M_{\mathrm{IEF},i}=a_i a_i^\top.
\]
Thus OPG couples each sensitivity direction to the squared output-loss-gradient scale, whereas undamped IEF keeps the direction-only outer product. Relative damping multiplies IEF by $s_i/(s_i+\tau_A)$ and therefore retains the near-zero qualification from Section~10.

For intuition, suppose a fraction $\varepsilon$ of samples are contaminated, their normalized sensitivity is concentrated near a direction $u$ outside the leading clean subspace, and their scale is $s_i\approx W$. If clean scales are sufficiently concentrated or independent of direction, then
\[
\mathbb E[M_{\mathrm{OPG}}]
\approx
(1-\varepsilon)\bar s_{\mathrm{clean}}\Sigma_{\mathrm{clean}}
+\varepsilon Wuu^\top,
\]
whereas undamped IEF has no factor $W$ on the contaminating direction. In particular, a sufficiently large $\varepsilon W$ can move $u$ into the leading OPG eigenspace. This model is illustrative rather than a distribution-free robustness theorem.

We test the mechanism directly in a fitted two-class MLP. For each seed, 512 inputs are drawn from $\mathcal N(0,I_2)$ and assigned the clean label $\mathbf{1}\{x_1+0.75x_2<0\}$; a uniformly selected $0$, $5$, $10$, or $20\%$ of labels is flipped. A $2$--$32$--$2$ tanh MLP is trained for 250 epochs with Adam, learning rate $0.01$, and batch size 64. For each of 20 independent seeds we then retain every per-sample parameter-gradient row and clean/corrupt identity. We decompose the full OPG and task-relative-damped IEF matrices ($\kappa=0.01$) into clean and corrupt contributions, then compare each total matrix with its clean-contribution reference. Table~\ref{tab:contamination-mechanism} reports means over seeds.

These observations support the proposed contamination mechanism in this controlled construction. They do not establish a universal robustness guarantee or prove that a diagonal continual-learning penalty improves on a real benchmark; those are separate empirical questions.
