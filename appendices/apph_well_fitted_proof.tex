\section{Proof of the Well-Fitted Scaling Proposition}
\label{app:well-fitted-proof}

For $s_n>0$, define
\[
\hat r_n^{,G}
=
\frac{r_n}{\sqrt{s_n}},
\qquad
b_n^{,G}
=
\|J_n^\top\hat r_n^{,G}\|_2^2.
\]
Direct substitution gives
\[
S_{n,0}^{\mathrm{IEF},G}
=
J_n^\top\hat r_n^{,G}(\hat r_n^{,G})^\top J_n,
\qquad
S_n^{\mathrm{OPG}}
=
s_nS_{n,0}^{\mathrm{IEF},G},
\]
and therefore
\[
\|S_{n,0}^{\mathrm{IEF},G}\|_2=b_n^{,G},
\qquad
\|S_n^{\mathrm{OPG}}\|_2=s_nb_n^{,G}.
\]
Since $G_n\preceq M_GI$, we have
\[
\|\hat r_n^{,G}\|_2^2
=
\frac{\|r_n\|_2^2}{r_n^\top G_n^{-1}r_n}
\le M_G.
\]
The bounded-Jacobian assumption consequently gives
$b_n^{,G}\le C_J^2M_G$, which proves the two upper bounds. The
nondegeneracy condition $\|J_n^\top\hat r_n^{,G}\|_2\ge c_J$ gives
the corresponding lower bounds.

For Euclidean softmax cross-entropy with correct-class probability
$p_{n,c}=1-\varepsilon$, the non-target probabilities sum to
$\varepsilon$, and hence
\[
\varepsilon^2
\le
\|p_n-y_n\|_2^2
=
\varepsilon^2+\sum_{k\ne c}p_{n,k}^2
\le
2\varepsilon^2.
\]
Thus $s_n\in\Theta(\varepsilon^2)$, which gives the stated rates under
the same nondegeneracy condition.

For positive damping, multiplying and dividing by $s_n$ yields
\[
S_{n,\tau}^{\mathrm{IEF},G}
=
\frac{s_n}{s_n+\tau}S_{n,0}^{\mathrm{IEF},G}.
\]
With fixed $\tau>0$, bounded $J_n$ gives
$\|J_n^\top r_n\|_2^2/(s_n+\tau)\to0$ as $r_n\to0$. Finally, under
a global positive loss rescaling by $c$, both $r_n$ and
$g_n=J_n^\top r_n$ scale by $c$, while $s_n$ and the task-relative
$\tau_A$ scale by $c^2$. Their quotient is therefore unchanged.
