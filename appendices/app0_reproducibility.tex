\section{Reproducibility and Artifact Contents}
\label{app:reproducibility}

The supplementary archive supports two distinct reproduction tasks. First,
all paper tables, intervals, and figures can be regenerated on CPU from the
preserved analysis-complete records without downloading benchmark data or
rerunning model training. Second, the model results can be rerun from the
frozen source after obtaining the datasets and model checkpoints under their
original terms. The archive contains the exact run-source snapshot and Git
history, a later reproduction-source snapshot, the manuscript source, the
protocol and its amendments, the canonical manifest and selected-lambda
artifacts with SHA-256 sidecars, validation and locked-test records, sensitivity
records, mechanism diagnostics, reporting scripts, a pinned dependency
lockfile, and file-level checksums. A bundle verifier checks counts, paths,
hashes, source ancestry, protocol identity, and the absence of restricted
example payloads before accepting an extracted archive.

\paragraph{Environment and execution.}
All confirmatory records identify the clean source commit
\texttt{5e170ea7d84d7094dd1734ee1516b9fba2133a63} and the same canonical
protocol-manifest hash. The environment is reconstructed with
\texttt{uv sync --frozen --dev}; the frozen source passes 75 CPU tests. Runs
enable deterministic PyTorch algorithms, disable cuDNN benchmarking and TF32,
and separately record model, training-loader, importance-order, and
model-Fisher sampling seeds. Each task reseeds training before constructing its
data loader, preventing method-specific importance computation from changing a
later task's minibatch order. Result files are written atomically. Validation
selection rejects missing or duplicate cells, mixed commits, manifest
mismatches, and locked records; locked evaluation requires the frozen selected
lambda artifact and records access before loading test examples.

The confirmatory suite comprises 192 validation cells, 176 locked-test cells,
and 48 validation-only sensitivity cells. ImageNet-R uses ten paired locked
class orders. TRACE uses all six permutations of its three retained tasks,
crossed with two optimizer seeds; the analysis additionally aggregates within
task order to avoid treating the twelve cells as twelve independent order
draws. The direct contamination experiment uses twenty independent seeds at
each of four corruption levels. Every prespecified cell is retained regardless
of effect direction. The analysis reports paired differences, Student-$t$
intervals, exact sign-flip tests where enumerable, the predeclared practical
threshold, and the complete prespecified secondary family.

\paragraph{Data and model provenance.}
The archive redistributes no benchmark images, language examples, or pretrained
weights. ImageNet-R is obtained from its authors; split definitions are pinned
to Mammoth commit \texttt{e75a491c69fd729edeb01431afb753d9157d9a81}.
The frozen roles contain 19,206 training, 6,000 validation, and 4,794 locked-test
images across 200 classes. The ViT-B/16 checkpoint is pinned to Hugging Face
revision \texttt{063c6c38a5d8510b2e57df480445e94b231dad2c}. TRACE is obtained
from its authors under the Apache-2.0 repository license. Its distributed
train, evaluation, and test files define the three data roles for C-STANCE,
ScienceQA, and NumGLUE-cm; the manifest hashes every role and verifies zero
prompt and prompt--answer overlap. FOMC is excluded because its distributed
evaluation and test files are identical. Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct is pinned to
revision \texttt{aa8e72537993ba99e69dfaafa59ed015b17504d1} and remains subject
to its model terms. Mammoth is MIT licensed, and the pinned \texttt{timm}
checkpoint declares Apache-2.0. Original IEWC source is supplied for review;
the submission does not override third-party terms or redistribute their
assets.

\paragraph{Compute and complexity.}
The confirmatory experiments ran on one workstation with an Intel Core
i9-13900K CPU and one NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5090 with 32~GiB of memory. Every run
records total wall time, and every importance phase records wall time and peak
CUDA allocation. With $d$ trainable parameters and $N$ retained importance
samples, diagonal uniform OPG and IEWC store $O(d)$ values and require $N$
per-sample gradients. RDW maintains at most $K$ unit residual directions on
CPU, using $O(Kd)$ additional storage and $O(NKd)$ inner-product work before
the same diagonal aggregation. The archived efficiency table reports the
measured cost of every locked method in both domains.

\paragraph{Regeneration checks.}
The reproduction guide gives exact commands to rebuild the locked analysis and
all generated LaTeX, PDF, PNG, and machine-readable reporting artifacts in a
fresh directory. Their checksum manifest is byte-reproducible after removal of
volatile PDF timestamps. A clean-checkout audit reconstructs the environment,
runs the test suite, regenerates the analysis and paper artifacts, compiles the
manuscript, and compares the resulting checksums. Historical multi-domain
evidence is retained through the complete 635-outcome ledger and path-level
checksums; records that may contain third-party prompts or targets are not
redistributed.
