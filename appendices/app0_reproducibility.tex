\section{Reproducibility and Artifact Contents}
\label{app:reproducibility}

The supplementary archive supports two reproduction paths. First, all paper
tables, intervals, and figures can be regenerated on CPU from the saved result
records, without downloading benchmark data or retraining models. Second, the
model results can be rerun from the archived source after obtaining the
datasets and model checkpoints under their original terms. The archive
contains the source snapshot used for all runs with its Git history, a later
snapshot used for reproduction, the manuscript source, the evaluation protocol, the selected hyperparameters,
validation and test records, sensitivity records, mechanism diagnostics,
reporting scripts, a pinned dependency lockfile, and SHA-256 checksums for
every file. A verification script checks file counts, paths, hashes, and source
commits, and confirms that no restricted dataset examples are included.

The empirical scripts use one \texttt{IEWCConfig} interface across the diagonal
and low-rank implementations. The same interface records output geometry,
damping, aggregation weights, and matrix approximation choices, and is used by
both the experiment and reproduction entry points.

\paragraph{Environment and execution.}
All held-out evaluation records come from source commit
\texttt{5e170ea7d84d7094dd1734ee1516b9fba2133a63}. The environment is
reconstructed with \texttt{uv sync --frozen --dev}, and the source passes 75 CPU
tests. Runs enable deterministic PyTorch algorithms, disable cuDNN benchmarking
and TF32, and separately record model, data-loader, importance-order, and
model-Fisher sampling seeds. Each task reseeds training before constructing its
data loader, so method-specific importance computation cannot change a later
task's minibatch order. Hyperparameter selection rejects missing or duplicate
runs and runs from mismatched commits, and test examples are loaded only after
the selected hyperparameters have been saved, with every test access logged.

The held-out evaluation comprises 192 validation runs, 176 test runs, and 48
validation-only sensitivity runs. ImageNet-R uses ten paired class orders.
TRACE uses all six permutations of its three tasks, crossed with two optimizer
seeds; the analysis additionally averages within task order so that the twelve
runs are not treated as twelve independent order draws. The contamination
experiment uses twenty independent seeds at each of four corruption levels.
Every planned run is reported regardless of the direction of its effect. The
analysis reports paired differences, Student-$t$ intervals, exact sign-flip
tests where enumerable, a one-point practical threshold fixed in advance, and
all Holm-corrected secondary comparisons.

\paragraph{Data and model provenance.}
The archive redistributes no benchmark images, language examples, or pretrained
weights. ImageNet-R is obtained from its authors; split definitions are pinned
to Mammoth commit \texttt{e75a491c69fd729edeb01431afb753d9157d9a81}.
The splits contain 19,206 training, 6,000 validation, and 4,794 test images
across 200 classes. The ViT-B/16 checkpoint is pinned to Hugging Face
revision \texttt{063c6c38a5d8510b2e57df480445e94b231dad2c}. TRACE is obtained
from its authors under the Apache-2.0 repository license. Its distributed
train, evaluation, and test files define the three splits for C-STANCE,
ScienceQA, and NumGLUE-cm; their hashes are recorded, and the splits share no
prompts or prompt--answer pairs. FOMC is excluded because its distributed
evaluation and test files are identical. Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct is pinned to
revision \texttt{aa8e72537993ba99e69dfaafa59ed015b17504d1} and remains subject
to its model terms. Mammoth is MIT licensed, and the pinned \texttt{timm}
checkpoint declares Apache-2.0. The IEWC source is supplied for review;
the submission does not override third-party terms or redistribute their
assets.

\paragraph{Compute and complexity.}
The held-out experiments ran on one workstation with an Intel Core
i9-13900K CPU and one NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5090 with 32~GiB of memory. Every run
records total wall time, and every importance phase records wall time and peak
CUDA allocation. With $d$ trainable parameters and $N$ importance samples,
diagonal uniform OPG and IEWC store $O(d)$ values and require $N$
per-sample gradients. RDW maintains at most $K$ unit residual directions on
CPU, using $O(Kd)$ additional storage and $O(NKd)$ inner-product work before
the same diagonal aggregation. The archive includes a table of the measured
cost of every method in both domains.

\paragraph{Regeneration checks.}
The reproduction guide gives exact commands to rebuild the held-out analysis
and all generated tables and figures in a fresh directory. Their checksums are
identical across rebuilds once volatile PDF timestamps are removed. A
clean-checkout test reconstructs the environment, runs the test suite,
regenerates the analysis and paper figures, compiles the manuscript, and
compares the resulting checksums. For the multi-domain study, the archive
includes the complete log of 635 development runs with per-file checksums;
records that may contain third-party prompts or targets are not
redistributed.
