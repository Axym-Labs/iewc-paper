\section{Additional Output-Space Derivations}

\subsection{Matched-metric hyperplane projection}
\label{app:matched-metric-projection}

For the tangent hyperplane $T=\{t:r^\top t=0\}$, write the normal residual as $e=\Delta z-t$ with $t\in T$. The projection solves
\[
\min_e e^\top G e
\quad\text{subject to}\quad
r^\top e=r^\top\Delta z.
\]
The Lagrange equations give
\[
e^\star
=
\frac{r^\top\Delta z}{r^\top G^{-1}r}G^{-1}r,
\]
whose squared $G$-norm is
\[
\|e^\star\|_G^2
=
\frac{(r^\top\Delta z)^2}{r^\top G^{-1}r}.
\]
Hence $G$ measures primal output displacement and $G^{-1}$ acts on the loss covector.

\subsection{Scalar least-squares reduction}
\label{app:scalar-gauss-newton}

Consider scalar-output least-squares regression in Euclidean geometry,
\[
\ell_n(z)=\frac12(z-y_n)^2.
\]
Then $r_n=z_n^A-y_n$ is scalar and, for $r_n\neq0$, the undamped normalized factor is $r_n^2/r_n^2=1$. Each IEF summand therefore reduces to $J_n^\top J_n$, and averaging gives
\[
M_{\mathrm{IEF}}
=
\frac{1}{N}\sum_{n=1}^N J_n^\top J_n.
\]
This is exactly the Gauss--Newton or output-Jacobian form for squared-error regression \citep{GaussNewton,MartensHF,MartensNG}, as also made explicit by \citet{IEF}.
