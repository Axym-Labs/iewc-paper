\section{Experimental Protocol, Provenance, and Sensitivity}

\subsection{Frozen confirmatory protocol}

The confirmatory protocol was serialized and hashed before any new locked-test model outcome was computed. It fixes data roles, methods, grids, repetitions, estimands, uncertainty calculations, and stopping rules. Locked-test loading requires the manifest hash and writes an append-only access record before loading examples. Validation scripts reject a locked-test role, and validation repetitions are excluded from confirmatory intervals.

The primary contrast in each domain is paired final average performance of uniform IEF minus uniform OPG. The secondary factorial comparison is
\[
\{\mathrm{OPG},\mathrm{IEF}\}
\times
\{\mathrm{uniform},\mathrm{RDW}\}.
\]
All four cells use the same retained examples, and the RDW cells receive the same unit-normalized parameter-gradient directions. Sampled model-Fisher EWC and MAS \citep{MAS} are external regularization baselines. Sequential and offline/joint training are references. Every nonzero diagonal importance is rescaled to mean one across trainable parameters, and every method uses the displayed $\lambda/2$ convention.

This estimator-focused factorial keeps the same post-task diagonal-penalty interface. SI's trajectory accumulation and LwF's distillation objective are discussed in Section~\ref{sec:related-work} but are not mixed into that factorial. Paper-facing EWC, IEWC, and IEWC+RDW correspond respectively to archived EF-EWC, IEWC-E/U-IEWC, and IEWC-GSS/GSS-IEWC labels.

\subsubsection{Residual-diversity weighting rule}
\label{app:rdw-rule}

For each nonzero parameter gradient, let
\[
u_n=\frac{g_n}{\|g_n\|_2}.
\]
In a fixed sample order, RDW maintains an orthonormal basis $Q$ of at most $K$ earlier residual directions and assigns
\[
h_n=\|(I-Q^\top Q)u_n\|_2^2.
\]
When the residual is nonzero and the cap is not reached, its normalized direction is appended to $Q$; the final $h_n$ are mean-normalized. This streaming rule is motivated by residual-gradient diversity ideas in GSS \citep{GSS}, but it is an order-dependent aggregation heuristic, not part of the level-set derivation.

ImageNet-R uses the fixed $\lambda$ grid $\{0.01,0.03,0.1,0.3,1\}$. TRACE uses $10^{-5}\!\times\!\{1,3,10,30,100\}$. Neither grid permits edge expansion.

ImageNet-R uses a pretrained ViT-B/16 with LoRA rank 4 and ten 20-class tasks, trained for two epochs per task with AdamW at learning rate $3\cdot10^{-4}$ and batch size 64. Importance uses 128 examples per completed task and RDW cap 64. TRACE uses Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct with LoRA rank 8, two epochs, AdamW at $2\cdot10^{-4}$, batch size 2 with gradient accumulation 8, 512 training examples per task, and 64 importance examples per completed task. Data, loader, optimizer, task-order, importance-order, and sampled-Fisher-label seeds are separately recorded and paired where appropriate.

Confirmatory execution uses explicit DataLoader generators, reseeds each task's training stage independently of intervening importance computation, and requires deterministic PyTorch and CUDA kernels. Before restarting validation, two complete same-configuration runs in each domain were compared after removing wall-clock metadata. Their training losses, evaluation matrices, and, for TRACE, generated example-level predictions matched exactly. These duplicate checks are retained for reproducibility auditing but excluded from selection.

For every paired contrast we report the mean and standard deviation of paired differences, a two-sided 95\% Student-$t$ interval, the exact two-sided sign-flip randomization $p$-value when enumerable, the median, and every paired point. These summaries treat task-order/seed pairs as independent; the interval assumes approximately normal paired differences, and sign-flip validity assumes sign exchangeability under the null. The primary interpretation uses a predeclared practical-difference threshold of $0.01$---one percentage point on either zero-to-one final-average metric---rather than a binary significance rule. A pre-lock audit found that the frozen protocol listed five secondary contrasts but called them four; the dated clarification applies Holm correction to all five within a domain. Full five-point validation curves, importance time, total run time, and peak CUDA memory are retained.

TRACE's twelve cells form a balanced cross of six task permutations and two model/optimizer seeds, so they are not twelve independent order draws. A further dated pre-lock amendment therefore leaves the twelve-cell primary analysis, practical threshold, five secondary contrasts, and Holm family unchanged, while adding two dependence checks. First, we average each method over the two seeds within an order and recompute all paired summaries over the six order-level units. Second, we average over all six orders within each seed and report the two seed-level paired differences descriptively, without treating a two-unit interval as reliable inference. The balanced construction preserves the overall paired mean. If the run-level and order-level uncertainty readings differ, the more cautious order-level reading governs claims about TRACE robustness. The six orders are the complete frozen permutation set, not a sample supporting extrapolation to an unlimited population of orders.

IEF uses task-relative damping
\[
\tau_A=10^{-2}\operatorname{median}\{s_i:s_i>0\}.
\]
Validation-only sensitivity varies the coefficient over $\{0,10^{-3},10^{-2},10^{-1},1\}$, importance counts, RDW caps, and RDW sample order. A separate loss-unit diagnostic multiplies a fitted classification loss by $0.1$, $1$, and $10$. OPG raw scale changes quadratically, fixed numerical damping changes IEF shape, and task-relative damping preserves the IEF matrix to numerical tolerance, matching Section~\ref{sec:well-fitted}.

The original search used a five-point multiplicative grid around a task-specific midpoint and extended an edge up to three times when an endpoint was best. This workflow supports descriptive selected-performance claims but not a selection-independent effect estimate. The revised protocol fixes all five points before validation, forbids edge expansion, and accesses the locked test only after a selection artifact is frozen.

The retained development ledger makes the broader selection history explicit rather than indexing only the five paper-facing settings. It contains 635 per-$\lambda$ outcomes: 148 vision-classification, 161 forecasting/regression, 47 DDPM, 55 segmentation, 110 encoder-language, and 114 TRACE decoder-only runs. The record includes pilots, smoke checks, tuning sweeps, selected-seed repetitions, and negative or mixed configurations across CIFAR-100 and ImageNet-R; ETT, M4, and long-horizon forecasting; synthetic and GLUE-family language sequences; three Qwen scales on TRACE; VOC 15--5; and MNIST DDPM. It also retains sequential, OPG-EWC, EWC-DR, uniform IEWC, residual-diversity, and FROMP-derived variants where implemented. Thus Table~\ref{tab:main-empirical-results} presents attained performance in the strongest representative multi-domain settings, while the archival ledger exposes the full development record and the new locked study supplies selection-independent comparisons.

\subsection{Damping and summand diagnostics}

Figure~\ref{fig:damping-sensitivity} combines two complementary damping diagnostics on a common higher-is-better change scale. Panel A re-expresses the historical fixed-$\tau$ CIFAR-100/FACIL prefix in changes from $\tau=10^{-2}$: the two curves are final accuracy and negative forgetting, so values below zero indicate worse performance on either measure. Panel B reports the predeclared relative-$\kappa$ sweep at the selected $\lambda$ on one recorded validation repetition per domain, relative to the confirmatory default $\kappa=10^{-2}$. The default is not uniformly best in these descriptive cells: $\kappa=1$ changes validation performance by $+0.0263$ on ImageNet-R and $+0.0007$ on TRACE. The former is material under the prespecified one-point threshold while the latter is negligible. These sensitivity outcomes motivate reporting and tuning damping rather than replacing the frozen confirmatory setting post hoc. Fixed $\tau$ has loss-dependent units; relative $\kappa$ is dimensionless and preserves global loss-unit invariance.
